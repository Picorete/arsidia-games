Prison Intake Officer

Inspection Simulator

For iPhone and iPad. Coming soon to the App Store.

Run the intake desk. Inspect every inmate, check the details, and decide where they belong.

Inspect every detail

Use manual search, scanners, a metal detector, UV light, and document checks. Some cases are straightforward. Others hide small inconsistencies that reward attention.

Make the call

Assign each inmate to General Population, High Security, the Medical Unit, or Observation. A rushed decision can affect the outcome of the shift.

Upgrade your station

Earn cash and improve your inspection tools. Better equipment helps you work faster and handle more demanding cases.

Endless overtime

Complete the first chapter and keep working procedurally generated overtime shifts with new combinations of cases, rules, and modifiers.
