Privacy Policy: Prison Intake Officer

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how the game Prison Intake Officer (the "game"), made by Arsidia Games LLC ("we", "us"), handles your information. In short: we do not collect or sell personal data ourselves. From version 1.0.1 the game shows ads, and our advertising partners collect some device information to show and measure them, as explained below. The game has no account.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, age, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game does not send your progress or gameplay to us. The advertising software in the game sends some device information to our advertising partners (see Ads and tracking).

What stays on your device

  • Your progress. Days played, cash, rank, upgrades and settings (sound, vibration, language) are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Technical logs. The game keeps a small local log of gameplay events and, if the game crashes, a crash report. They help with troubleshooting on the device. In this version they are never sent anywhere: they stay on your device and are removed when you delete the game.
  • Deleting the game removes all of this. You can also erase your progress at any time in Settings > Reset progress.
  • If your device backs up app data (for example to iCloud or to a computer), your progress may be included in that backup. Apple and your device settings control those backups; we never receive them.

Internet connection

The game works offline. When it is online it downloads ads and a small settings file from our website (game settings only, nothing about you is sent). If you open a web link from the game (such as this policy), it opens in your web browser, which is governed by the browser's and the website's own policies.

Ads and tracking

  • The game shows ads: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and a short ad between shifts from time to time. No ads play during a shift or in the first day of play.
  • Ads are served through Google AdMob and the ad networks that bid through it. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with the ads. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks for permission (after a short explanation) before any app tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads. You can review or withdraw it at any time in the game's Settings > Privacy choices.
  • If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, use Settings > Report an ad in the game, or write to us.
  • The game has no in-app purchases. We do not sell personal data and do not share it with data brokers.

Device permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts or notifications.

Information Apple may share with us

The game is distributed through the App Store, which Apple operates under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports about the game. This sharing is controlled by Apple and by your settings, not by the game, and we use it only to fix problems and improve the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow our advertising partners to collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Privacy laws in your region (such as the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA) may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. We do not hold personal data from the game ourselves; for data our advertising partners collected, contact us and we will point you to the right partner, or use the choices above. You can always delete everything the game stored by erasing your progress or deleting the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles data, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above. If a change requires your consent, the game will ask for it first.
